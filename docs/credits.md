# Credits

## The team

Team SHDC had three members. By Oscar's account, his two teammates coded the tutorial and rounds 1 to 3, Oscar coded rounds 4 and 5, and he verified the team's results. Teammates stay anonymous.

## Tools the team used

Per the audit of the team's code (Codex, 2026), relayed; original files not published:

- **Visualiser:** [gsgill7](https://github.com/gsgill7)'s fork of [jmerle](https://github.com/jmerle)'s Prosperity visualiser. jmerle's open backtester and visualiser go back to [Prosperity 2](https://github.com/jmerle/imc-prosperity-2).
- **Rust backtester:** by [GeyzsoN](https://github.com/GeyzsoN).

## Used after the competition

- **Log format:** the post-competition lab reads logs in the format of [nabayansaha/imc-prosperity-4-backtester](https://github.com/nabayansaha/imc-prosperity-4-backtester), pinned at revision `0094c681f8cd019889761e6431a1a47ea151aaa8`. No upstream code is copied ([after-the-competition.md](after-the-competition.md)).
- **Reservation price:** Avellaneda, M. and Stoikov, S., [High-frequency trading in a limit order book](https://math.nyu.edu/inmemoriam/avellaneda/HighFrequencyTrading.pdf).
